# Crit 8 Reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I was unable to complete the Crit 8 prototype by the original deadline because I was unwell. I therefore cannot claim that I had a working deployed application at that point.

However, this setback made me reconsider the scope of ANU TaskMate. My original idea covered paid assistance, study groups, social connections, and campus activities, which was ambitious for an initial prototype. I realised that trying to build everything together would make it harder to deliver something usable.

When I resumed work for Crit 9, I focused first on the core journey: creating a post, viewing opportunities, applying, and keeping changes in sync between users. That smaller starting point made it possible to develop the platform in stages.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Crit 8 reminded me that having a meaningful idea is not the same as delivering a working product.

I want to become a developer who can adapt when circumstances change, recognise what matters most to users, and define a realistic minimum version without losing the original purpose.

I also learned that honest documentation matters: an incomplete milestone should be acknowledged rather than presented as finished.
